It’s our last day in London and it’s raining outside.

I haven’t stayed in London for long, but in this foreign land I can feel the sincerity and kindness here. Not all black people are ruffians, and not all white people are alcoholics — and the same goes for us Orientals. There is no prejudice against Europeans. During the two months in London I felt more politeness and respect than we may ever get used to at home.

I am very fortunate that at almost thirty I can come to a foreign country. There is a joke I saw: the universe is so big, but you can’t get out of this mere 9.6 million square kilometers. So whenever we get the chance, let’s come out together and experience the exotic customs.
