I’m a programmer working on blockchain. I’m familiar with the EVM and with networks like Ethereum L2, Tron and BSC, and I’ve shipped NFT marketplaces, multisig wallets, ENS, DAOs and decentralized payments on them.

I’m not attached to any single programming language — a language is only a tool. What matters is understanding the business well enough to pick the one that fits it best, whether that’s Rust, C, Typescript or Solidity. Most of my side projects live on Github.

I care about how the work gets done as much as about the code itself. TDD and agile practice keep the feedback loop short and the design honest, which is exactly what lets me get the work finished faster rather than slower.

I’m an explorer at heart. I love wandering aimlessly through the streets and alleys of small towns — it’s a lot like life itself: you’re always walking some road, and then suddenly the way ahead is gone, so you grope around, backtrack, and end up finding a different path entirely. I find all of that interesting.
